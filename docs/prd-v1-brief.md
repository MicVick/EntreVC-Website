EntreVC Website and Portal 
Product Requirements Document  |  Version 1.0  |  Entrepreneurship and Venture Capital Club, IIM Ahmedabad 
1. Objective 
Build a single public home for EntreVC that replaces scattered posters, forms and message threads. The site should let a visitor 
understand what the club does, register for an event, browse the startups built by students and alumni, and access the club’s 
knowledge assets without contacting anyone. Every piece of content is owned and published by the club team through an 
admin portal, so the site can be run for years without developer support. 
2. Users 
– Students: current and prospective students looking for events, resources and co-founders 
– Alumni: alumni founders who want visibility, and who the club wants to keep connected 
– External: investors, mentors and speakers who need a credible view of the ecosystem on campus 
– Internal: the club team, who must publish and update content quickly during the year 
3. Core scope 
Module 
Requirements 
Priority 
Home 
Positioning statement and short intro to the club. Strip of the next three events with a direct register 
link. Featured startups and latest resource. Key numbers (events held, startups listed, alumni 
network). Mailing list signup. Clear entry points into every other section. Footer with social links. 
P0 
Events 
Upcoming and past event listings. Detail page with images, date, venue, format, speakers, agenda 
and registration deadline. Registration form with capacity limits, confirmation email and calendar 
file. Past events archived with recap, photographs and session recordings. 
P0 
Startups 
Separate views for alumni ventures and current student ventures. Card with logo, short description, 
founders, batch, sector, stage and links. Filters and search by sector, stage and batch. All listings are 
created and maintained by the club team. 
Also add a section of IIMA Ventures – details, contact, highlight and most importantly various 
schemes available for IIMA students. 
P0 
Resources 
The EntreVC startup playbook section with a downloadable version. Curated library of articles, 
books, podcasts and reports, each tagged and searchable. Embedded feed from the club YouTube 
channel. Option to gate selected material behind an email signup. 
P0 
Team 
Current team with role, photograph, batch and LinkedIn. Visual of the club structure and the 
verticals under it. Archive of past teams. 
P0 
Contact 
Dedicated page with the club mail id, campus address and social links. Role specific contacts for 
sponsorship, speaker invitations and media. General query form with a category selector (general, 
event, startup listing, sponsorship, speaking, mentorship), name, email, batch or organisation and 
message. Automatic acknowledgement to the sender and routing to the correct vertical. 
P0 
Admin 
portal 
Role based login for the team. Create, edit and publish events, startups, resources and team profiles 
without touching code. Draft and scheduled publishing with a review step. View, filter and export 
event registrations. Inbox for query form submissions. Mailing list export. Media library. Access 
managed by role, so annual handover is a transfer of roles rather than shared passwords. 
P0 
4. Additional recommendations 
1. EntreFair board: All process of EntreFair Conducted via this. 
2. Mentor and investor connect: 1:1 Slot booking pipeline 
3. Blog Section : a light blog and newsletter archive, so long form writing by members has a permanent home. 
4. Global Search: site wide search 
5. Technical and operational requirements 
1. Stack: Dynamic framework such as Next.js + Firebase with a CMS, deployed on a managed host. 
2. Performance: largest contentful paint under 2.5 seconds on 4G, and full responsiveness from 360px upward. 
3. Data and compliance: registration data stored with consent, no personal contact details published without written approval. 
4. Analytics and sharing: page level analytics, structured metadata and a custom preview image for every event and startup 
page, since most links will be shared on WhatsApp and LinkedIn.
